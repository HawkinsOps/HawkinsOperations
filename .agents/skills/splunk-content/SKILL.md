# Splunk Content

## Use when

- deciding where Splunk proof should live
- updating Splunk wording on public pages
- packaging Splunk evidence into reviewer-safe artifacts

## Workflow

1. Read `docs/PRECEDENCE_CONTRACT.md` first.
2. Treat committed repo-side exports as the authority for claim ceilings.
3. Keep Splunk as supporting investigation proof unless evidence volume justifies a larger surface.
4. Avoid wording that implies analyst-workflow maturity without a committed walkthrough artifact.

## Output expectations

- claim ceiling
- placement recommendation
- evidence gaps
- packaging plan
