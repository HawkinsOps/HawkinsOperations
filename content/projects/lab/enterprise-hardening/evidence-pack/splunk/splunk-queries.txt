## Saved Search: AuditPolicyChange_4719
## Purpose: Find audit policy changes — subcategory and the exact change text.
## Time range: Last 24 hours (or narrow to hardening window)

sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=4719
| rex "Subcategory:\\s+(?<subcategory>.+)"
| rex "Changes:\\s+(?<changes>.+)"
| table _time host ComputerName SubjectUserName subcategory changes Message
| sort - _time


## Saved Search: ProcessCreation_CommandLine_Missing
## Purpose: Find 4688 events with missing CommandLine (pre-hardening baseline)

sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=4688
| eval cmd=coalesce(CommandLine, Command_Line, ProcessCommandLine, New_Process_CommandLine, "")
| where cmd="" OR isnull(cmd) OR cmd=="-"
| stats count AS events_without_cmd BY ComputerName, NewProcessName, host
| sort - events_without_cmd


## Saved Search: ProcessCreation_CommandLine_Present
## Purpose: Confirm CommandLine field populated after hardening

sourcetype="WinEventLog:Security" EventCode=4688
| eval cmd=coalesce(CommandLine, Command_Line, ProcessCommandLine, New_Process_CommandLine, "")
| where NOT (cmd="" OR isnull(cmd) OR cmd=="-")
| table _time host ComputerName NewProcessName cmd
| sort - _time
