This is a working security operations system, not a static portfolio.

I design, build, and operate SignalFoundry: a production-grade detection-to-escalation pipeline across Sigma, Wazuh, and Splunk that automatically triages alerts, collects forensic evidence, and only escalates verified threats with full proof attached.

In 7 months, I transitioned from factory operations into cybersecurity and built a pipeline that has processed 55,665 verified cases with 140 custom detections (103 Sigma rules, 28 Wazuh blocks, 9 Splunk queries) while working 12-hour manufacturing shifts.

Factory-trained discipline meets modern SOC automation. Every metric is real, every detection is version-controlled, and every claim is reproducible.

If you're looking for someone who can actually build and run detection engineering and security automation, not just talk about it, you're in the right place.
