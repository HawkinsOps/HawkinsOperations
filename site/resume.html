SignalFoundry

What It Is

SignalFoundry is my flagship SOC triage pipeline built around consistent first-pass decisioning and evidence integrity.

What It Does

It ingests alerts, applies policy-driven triage, enforces redaction, and publishes escalation artifacts only after quality and reconciliation checks pass.

Why It Matters

It reduces analyst noise, preserves review quality, and gives hiring teams auditable proof that execution is repeatable, not slideware.
