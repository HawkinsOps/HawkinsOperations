Decision logic

Deterministic dispositions

SignalFoundry uses explicit dispositions to keep triage behavior reviewable and reproducible.

Disposition Meaning System action
AUTO_CLOSE_BENIGN Expected activity Close and log evidence summary
AUTO_CLOSE_KNOWN_FP Known false positive pattern Close, annotate reason, keep audit trail
ESCALATE Suspicious or high-confidence case Generate evidence pack and escalation artifacts
Operational value

What the workflow fixes

  • Standardizes evidence collection so handoff quality does not depend on analyst style.
  • Keeps redaction as a mandatory gate before any artifact becomes public.
  • Preserves the escalation path even while reducing false-positive pressure.
Escalation output

What the pipeline produces

Every escalated case generates a structured evidence pack. Five artifacts, each with a defined role in the review chain. All pass the mandatory redaction gate before publication.

Evidence pack structure
00_one_pager.md Intake summary for fast reviewer orientation
01_full_report.md Full investigative narrative with context
02_timeline.csv Machine-readable event chronology
03_queries.md Reproduction queries for the alert
04_closure_report.md Final disposition and audit trail
Workflow evidence

The operational surface that produces the evidence packs. Each artifact is generated programmatically, passes redaction, and is committed to the repository.
